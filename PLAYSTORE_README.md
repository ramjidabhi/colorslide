```ini

config/app_id="ca-app-pub-6827648916951660~3848367705"
config/rewarded_ad_id="ca-app-pub-6827648916951660/1711113358"
config/banner_id="ca-app-pub-6827648916951660/1711113358"
config/interstitial_id="ca-app-pub-6827648916951660/1711113358"
```

### Rewarded Video Ad Placements & Rewards:
1. **Special Features / Boosters (Level 5+)**:
   - When a player has 0 of a booster or cannot afford it with coins, they can tap to watch a short rewarded video ad.
   - **Reward**: Grants **+1 Free Booster** (🔨 Sledgehammer, ✂️ Shrink Ray, or 🧲 Crane Picker) and activates booster mode immediately!
2. **Helpful Walkthrough Hints (+1 Hint)**:
   - When out of hints or from the Shop Dialog, players can watch a rewarded video ad.
   - **Reward**: Grants **+1 Free Hint** (`gm.add_hints(1)`), unlocking the current level's walkthrough!

*AdMob Interstitials are frequency-capped to trigger every 5 levels cleared, preserving player retention.*

## 9. Android Export & Build Instructions

### Step 1: Keystore Generation
Run in PowerShell:
```powershell
keytool -genkey -v -keystore colorslide-release.keystore -alias colorslide -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000
```

### Step 2: Configure Export in Godot
1. Open Godot 4.7.2 Mono → **Project → Export...**
2. Select or Add **Android** Preset.
3. Under **Custom Build**: Enable Custom Build.
4. Under **Plugins**: Check `GodotGooglePlayBilling`, `GodotPlayGames`, `AdMob`.
5. Under **Keystore**:
   - Release Keystore: `res://colorslide-release.keystore`
   - Release User: `colorslide`
   - Release Password: `<your_password>`
6. Under **Architectures**: Enable `arm64-v8a` and `armeabi-v7a`.
7. Under **Export Format**: Select **Export AAB** (Android App Bundle).
8. Click **Export Project** → Save as `colorslide_release.aab`.
